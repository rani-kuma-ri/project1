The Tortoise and the Rabbit

One day, a fast and proud Rabbit was boasting about how quickly he could run. A slow and steady Tortoise grew tired of hearing him brag and challenged him to a race.
The Rabbit laughed at the idea but agreed. The race began, and the Rabbit quickly ran far ahead. Confident he would win, he decided to rest under a tree and soon fell asleep.

Meanwhile, the Tortoise kept moving—slowly but steadily—never stopping. When the Rabbit woke up, he saw the Tortoise near the finish line. The Rabbit ran as fast as he could, but it was too late.
The Tortoise crossed the finish line first and won the race.

moral of story

Slow and steady wins the race.
